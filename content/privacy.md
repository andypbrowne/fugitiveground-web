Last updated 12 September 2026.

This page covers the Fugitive Ground iOS app and this website. They are separate. The app does not send your location or journal to this site.

## This website

These pages are static. There are no accounts, forms, comments, or analytics pixels.

This site does not set cookies. The live sunset times on the home page run in your browser after you ask to use your location. Coordinates stay in the browser and are not sent to a server.

The site is hosted on [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/). Like most hosts, Netlify may keep ordinary request logs such as IP address, user agent, and the page requested, for security and operations. See [Netlify’s privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).

## The app

Fugitive Ground calculates a daily window from sunset at a place you choose.

### Location

Location is optional. If you allow it, the app uses it to calculate sunset where you are. You can also pick a place. The app does not send location to this website or to an analytics service.

The system prompt says: *Fugitive Ground uses your location to calculate the sunset window where you are.*

### Notifications

Notifications are optional and scheduled on the device. They remind you about the start of the window. The app does not send push messages through a server.

### Journal

Journal entries are stored on your device, tied to a date and place. They do not go to a Fugitive Ground server. This website never sees them.

### What the app does not do

The app does not use ad tracking. It does not sell personal data. It does not build an advertising profile.
